Ace Studio was founded by Bethany Ochs and Javier Fernandez as a photography studio and gathering space for local and visiting professional photographers. It’s an approachable and intentionally designed space with all the amenities to make your best creative work possible. In addition to being a photo studio, Ace Studio hosts workshops, lectures, film screenings, book readings, intimate musical performances and more.

The photo studio portion is 200 sq/ft and the 450 sq/ft living room area is equipped with ample seating, a projector, and speakers. A separate kitchenette and private bathrooms are also available.

Additional photography equipment is available for rent upon request.
